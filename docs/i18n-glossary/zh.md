# Chinese glossary — `bitshares-vanilla-ui`

This is the file an external auditor reads **before** trusting a machine-filled
Chinese translation. It fixes **one Chinese term per concept**, so a single
correction here (plus the same word in `vanilla/locales/zh.json`) propagates
everywhere instead of leaving three near-synonyms in the UI.

**How to use it**

- Translate the English term in column 1 as the Chinese term in column 2. Do not
  invent a second Chinese word for the same concept: a synonym split is the one
  mistake this file exists to prevent.
- Column 3 says why, and names the `zh.json` keys that already prove the wording.
  Keys cited there were read out of `vanilla/locales/zh.json` after the
  2026-10-13 chunk-C pass (`tooling/apply_i18n_zh_chunkC.py` filled the last 157
  signing/UX strings; 11 more stayed English on purpose and are listed in that
  script's `KEPT_ENGLISH`). A citation is evidence, not decoration: in the notes,
  a Chinese WORD in **bold** is a form under discussion, not a locale key — only
  things shaped like `section.key` are citations.
- "N strings" below means: N keys of `zh.json` whose value differs from
  `en.json` and contains the Chinese word, counted case-insensitively on the
  shipped file. A count is a smoke alarm for a synonym split, not a definition.
- Corrections land through the `translation` issue label on the project tracker —
  the same route `#/help/translation-limitations` in the wallet gives readers.
- **Status:** the Chinese column below is the agreed vocabulary, not yet a claim
  of human review. The Chinese fill cross-checked every row against `zh.json` and
  chose the form that file actually uses. Until a person has read a string in
  Chinese, the wallet labels it machine-filled — in the reader's own language
  (`i18n.badge_body`, 「这些译文由机器写成，未经人工核对。签名页面上标有 * 的行还会显示英文原文。」)
  — and every signing line marked `*` shows the English one tap away
  (`i18n.risk_show_english`, 「显示本行英文原文」).
- **Every row names the form `zh.json` uses.** Where a second form exists for the
  same concept, the row lists **every** string carrying it — as a counter-example
  (align it on the next pass) or as a same-root other sense (leave it alone). A
  glossary that breaks a dozen correct trading strings is worse than no glossary.
- **Typography.** `zh.json` carries NO ASCII apostrophe, NO typographic
  apostrophe (U+2019), NO ideographic space and NO Cyrillic letter in any value
  this project writes — Chinese needs none of them. The only apostrophes left in
  the file sit inside untranslated English. Chinese quotes with “ ”, which the
  gate does not touch. Do not "fix" an English apostrophe in by transliterating
  it: `vote.proxies_follow_another_account_s_slate_re` and
  `barter.two_sided_atomic_swap_preview_preview_first_t` rephrase the English
  possessive (双方 / 另一账户的) instead of borrowing it.
- **The brevity hazard.** Chinese is far more compact than English, and a
  signing dialog shows both lines. Where a short Chinese rendering could drop an
  amount, a direction, an irreversibility or a permission, this file's rows point
  at the fuller form on purpose: `trade.uc_total` is 卖出总量 (not 总计),
  `borrow.bids_no_cancel` keeps both 无法取消 and the two outcomes,
  `transfer.enclosed_op_note` keeps 仅在批准之后, and `vote.retried_prefix` /
  `vote.retry_self` say 以自己身份 so the reader can see whose stake signed.

Placeholders (`%(name)s`), URLs, object ids (`1.2.x`), asset symbols and theme
or network ids are **byte-verbatim** in every language and never appear here.
50 keys of `zh.json` are byte-identical to `en.json` on purpose: brands
(`shell.brand`, `shell.footer_brand*`, `menu.p_spotlight`), network/theme ids
(`settings.network_*`, `settings.theme_*`), chain acronyms (`HTLC`, `NFT`, `UIA`,
`PMA`, `MCR`, `MSSR`, `ID`, `URL`), indicator acronyms (`SMA`, `EMA`, `PSAR`,
`BB`), the «—»/«…» sentinels (`settings.dash`, `settings.pending`,
`market.stat_empty`, `assets_feed.s1`, `explorer_assets.s1`), template glue
(`market_net.edge_card`, `pool_net.edge_card`, `shell.footer_net_host`) and
example payloads the reader overwrites (`account_net.seeds_placeholder`,
`ticket.airdrop_example_ph`, `xrate.token_placeholder`).

| wallet | 钱包 | The local vault this app calls a wallet (`auth.create_a_local_wallet`, `auth.local_wallet_unlock_on_this_device`, `common.wallet_locked`), 189 strings. Never 「皮夹」/「钱包应用」 — the word is plain 钱包. | collateral | 抵押物 | Backing locked behind a smartcoin or a credit position (`borrow.hdr_collateral` 「抵押物」, `credit.collateral`, `account.margin_hint`), 36 strings. Never 「抵押品」 (0 strings) and never 「质押品」. Same root, other sense and correct as they stand — the coin a smartcoin is pegged to is the **备兑资产** on the asset page (`asset.backing_field` 「备兑资产（1.3.N）」) and the **支撑资产** in the prediction/borrow prose (`prediction.backing_asset` 「支撑资产」, `borrow.backing_ratio` 「备兑比率」, `borrow.is_backed_by` 「 的备兑资产为 」). *Backing* is the peg, *collateral* is the stake posted against a loan; `borrow.how_1` keeps both in one sentence (「锁定抵押物（例如 BTS）…针对智能币（例如 bitUSD）」), so they are never interchanged. |
| precision | 精度 | Decimal places an asset stores (`explorer.precision_row`, `explorer.th_precision`, `asset.precision_field` 「精度（0–12）」), 18 strings. Never retyped inside an amount string, and never translated away: `eslab.hint_donors` 「窗口期内的计数；合计为原始整数 — 请除以资产精度。」, `eslab.hint_holders`, `eslab.prec_unknown` 「精度未知 — 原始整数。」 Same root, other sense — **报价精度** stays the same word inside the compound: `explorer_assets.s2` 「不可用（计价精度未知）」, `explorer.unavailable_quote`. |
| share | 份额 | A generic fraction of something: a pool share (`help.topic_pools_text` 「质押资金池份额」, `prediction.prediction_market_assets_yes_no_shares_positi` 「预测市场资产（YES/NO 份额）」, `prediction.quick_position_hint` 「NO 花费份额」), a stake deposit's yield share (`explorer.no_pools_found` 「质押表单」), an index proportion (`eslab.hint_agg` 「占比为精确整数数学百分比」). Narrowed form, not a rival term — **LP 份额** is the pool token: `pool.shares_field` 「LP 份额」. Same root, other sense and correct as they stand — ODDS are **隐含概率** / **赔率**, not 份额: `prediction.probability` 「隐含概率」, `prediction.prob_american` 「美式赔率」. And **QUOTE**, the market pair's second asset, is 报价 — see *quote* below for the collision that word causes. |
| liquidity pool | 流动性资金池 | A CPMM market (`pools.title` 「流动性资金池」, `pool_detail.title`, `help.topic_pools_title`), 12 strings for the full form and 146 for **资金池** — the same word shortened, which is what the tables, the buttons and the pool network use (`pool_net.title` 「资金池网络图」, `pool_net.col_pool` 「资金池」, `nav.pools` 「资金池」, `pools.selector_title` 「资金池选择器」). One word in prose, the full gloss in titles — not two words. Never 「流动性储备」. Same root, other sense and correct as they stand — the FEE POOL is a NAMED CHAIN OBJECT and keeps both parts: `explorer.fee_pool` 「手续费池」, `account.op_fee_pool_fund` 「手续费池注资」, `account.sum_fee_pool_fund` 「已向 %(asset)s 的手续费池注资 %(amount)s」. |
| pool share | LP 份额 | What you hold in a liquidity pool (`pool.shares_field` 「LP 份额」). The plain **份额** in the same sense — `help.topic_pools_text`, `help.topic_pools_body` — is the same concept shortened, not a different term. A pool share is NOT the stake you deposit (next row) and NOT a **份额资产** (`pool.share_help`), which is the token, not your fraction of it. |
| stake | 质押 | The locked balance that earns voting weight or a pool's yield (`pool.list_sub`, `explorer.no_pools_found` 「质押表单」, `menu.d_tickets` 「投票锁定的质押，权重随之增长。」, `help.topic_tickets_text` 「锁定质押换随锁长增长的投票权重。」), 53 strings (21 pool/vote strings plus 32 voting-stake strings, which carry 质押 inside 投票质押). A pool share is not a **份额**, and one side of a swap is not a **分支** (`barter.no_legs` 「无分支」, `xrate.unpriced` 「此路径上的某一条分支…」). |
| market | 市场 | A base/quote trading pair (`nav.markets` 「市场」, `market.picker_title` 「市场」, `borrow.market`), 178 strings. Same root, other sense and correct as they stand — a PREDICTION market is **预测市场** (`prediction.prediction_markets`), and one graph step on a market or pool map is a **跳**, not a market: `account_net.depth_1` 「1 跳」, `pool_net.hops` 「%(n)s 跳到 BTS」, `xrate.no_route_col` 「6 跳资金池路径内没有通往 %(sym)s 的路线。」, `xrate.empty` 「本链上距 BTS 3 跳以内没有资金池资产。」 |
| exchange | 交易所 | The DEX venue (`nav.exchange`, `market_book.title`, `menu.p_exchange`, `trade.title`, `tour.away_note` 「在交易所交易台上…」), 32 strings. The acronym **DEX** itself stays Latin — `news.exchange_trade_on_the_dex` 「交易所 — 在 DEX 上交易」, `help.topic_dex-intro_body` 「去中心化交易所直接在链上买卖数字资产」, `help.topic_dex-trading_text`. Never 「兑换」 for the venue (that word is a *swap*, next rows) and never the Latin "Exchange" in chrome. Same root, other sense and correct as it stands — 交易所 inside `settings.theme_dex_dark` 「交易所深色」 is the THEME name for the same venue, so it is consistent, not a split. **Counter-example, and the sharpest one in this file:** the SAME English master, "Exchange", renders two different words — `market.title` is 「交易」 while `trade.title` and `market_book.title` are 「交易所」. Align `market.title` onto 交易所 on the next pass, together with whatever chrome reads it; do not align 交易所 down onto 交易, because `trade.chain_hint` and the op-label table already own 交易 for a *transaction*. |
| desk | 交易台 | The page a flow lives on (`market.exchange_desk` 「交易台」, `instant.open_the_full_desk` 「打开完整交易台」, `prediction.desk_suffix` 「 交易台」, `tour.away_note` 「在交易所交易台上…」, `menu.d_exchange`), 13 strings. | orderbook | 订单簿 | The DEX book (`market.order_book` 「订单簿」, `pool.book_title`, `market.raw_book` 「原始订单簿」, `instant.the_order_book_is_empty_type_a_price_manually`, `menu.d_exchange`), 26 strings. | order | 订单 | Anything resting on or filled from the book (`market.col_order`, `instant.order_prefix`, `trade.uc_orders` 「订单」), 93 strings. | fee | 手续费 | The chain fee an operation pays (`borrow.fee` 「手续费」, `vesting.fee`, `trollbox.fee`, `instant.fee`), 168 strings.
